This page explains how to delete an account in **Studafy**, the school app
made by **Studafy Limited**.

## In the app

1. Open Studafy and sign in to the account you want to delete.
2. Tap your profile picture to open **Account**, then open **Sign-in and
   security**, then **Delete account**.
3. Read what will be deleted and what your school keeps, confirm, and sign in
   again when asked.

A parent deletes a child account they manage in the same way, from the
child's account.

Before you delete, you can download a copy of your data: **Account**, then
**Download my data**.

## Without the app

Email **hello@studafy.com** from the email address you sign in to Studafy
with, with the subject "Delete my account". If you cannot send from that
address, tell us the address, and we will ask you to confirm in a way that
proves the account is yours. We never ask for your password. We reply within
one month.

## What happens next

- **For 14 days** the account can still be restored: sign in and cancel the
  deletion in the app, or email us.
- **After 14 days** the account is deleted. You can no longer sign in, and
  we:
  - erase your email address, name and sign-in details;
  - replace your name on your profile and on class rosters with "Deleted
    user" or "Deleted student", and clear your gender;
  - end your memberships, family links and conversations;
  - delete your devices and notification settings, notifications, consents,
    blocks, data exports, notebooks and study notebooks, AI usage records,
    and your personal photos (photos for the notebook or the study helper);
  - remove your email address from invitations.

## What is kept

Some records belong to your school or are kept by law, and stay after your
account is deleted, without your name:

- **school records:** marks and their history, attendance, work you handed
  in and its files, which your school may be required to keep;
- **messages you already sent**, which stay with the people you sent them
  to, shown as from "Deleted user";
- **audit and security records** of what happened in the app;
- **payment records**, for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Encrypted backups hold a copy for up to 30 days before they are deleted.
