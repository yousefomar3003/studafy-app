## 1. What Studafy is

Studafy is a school application. Teachers run classes, share lessons, set and
mark work — including work done on paper — and record attendance. Students
see and hand in their work and keep their own notes. Parents follow their
child's progress. Everyone can message the people in their classes.

This version of Studafy has three roles: **teacher**, **student** and
**parent or guardian**. **There is no school administrator.** A class's work,
marks and attendance are managed by that class's own teachers.

## 2. Accounts

- You sign in with Google, Microsoft or Apple. Keep that account secure; what
  is done with your Studafy account is your responsibility.
- You may add several accounts to one device and switch between them. Sign
  out of any account on a device you share.
- **Child accounts.** A parent or legal guardian may create a student account
  for a child who has no email or phone. By doing so, the parent confirms
  they are the child's parent or legal guardian, accepts these terms and the
  Privacy Policy on the child's behalf, and is responsible for how the
  account is used.
- A student may need a parent's approval to buy a subscription.
- A profile photo you add stays on your device; it is never uploaded.
- Give accurate information. Do not create an account for someone else
  except as a parent under this section, and do not impersonate anyone.

## 3. Using Studafy properly

You agree not to:

- use Studafy for anything other than learning, teaching and school
  communication;
- harass, bully, threaten or demean anyone, or share anything sexual,
  violent, hateful or unlawful;
- upload files you have no right to share, or that contain malware;
- try to see records you are not entitled to see — another student's marks
  or work, another class, another school — or to get around the app's limits;
- hand in another person's work, or work made by the AI tools, as your own;
- copy, scrape or resell the service or its content.

We may remove content and suspend or close accounts that break these rules,
particularly where a child's safety is at risk. Anyone can report a message
or a person, or block someone, from inside the app.

## 4. For teachers

- **You are responsible for the accuracy of the marks and attendance you
  record.** Marks you enter are drafts, visible only to your class's
  teachers, until you publish them. Publishing tells the students and their
  linked parents.
- **Corrections are recorded, not erased.** To change a published mark you
  must give a reason. The previous mark, the new one, who changed it, when
  and why are kept, and the family is told if the mark itself changed. Give
  honest reasons: they form part of the student's record.
- **Attendance.** You can change marks freely on the lesson's day. After
  that, changing a mark needs a reason. Seven days after the lesson the
  register locks; only the class's lead teacher can then change it, with a
  reason. Every change is kept.
- **Deadlines.** Due times are set in your school's time zone. You may give a
  student more time, or reopen work for the class, and should record why.
- **Lessons and material.** You may edit a lesson or replace its file, and
  change the files on set work. Files you replace or remove are kept for the
  school; the class no longer sees them.
- Share only material you have the right to share, and nothing about a
  student with anyone who has no need to know it.

## 5. For students

- Hand in your own work. You can replace your work until the deadline shown,
  which is in your school's time zone. Your earlier hand-ins are kept, and
  your teacher can see them.
- After the deadline you cannot hand in unless your teacher gives you more
  time. Once your teacher has published a mark for a piece of work, you cannot
  replace it unless your teacher lets you.
- If you think a mark or an attendance record is wrong, ask your teacher.

## 6. For parents and guardians

- You can link to your child only with your child's approval, unless you
  created your child's account.
- A linked parent can see their child's published marks, attendance, set work
  and hand-ins, and class material shared with families, and can download the
  files in them. You can hand work in for a young child who has no device of
  their own; the teacher will see that a parent handed it in.
- **You cannot change your child's marks or attendance**, and you are not
  given your child's private messages.
- **Family+** is an optional paid plan that you hold yourself. One plan
  covers up to 4 of your linked children and a larger plan up to 8, in the
  order you linked them. It summarises what you can already see about each
  child it covers, compares a child only with their own earlier record, and
  does not predict or diagnose anything.

## 7. Your content

You keep the rights you have in what you upload and write — work, notes,
photos, voice notes and messages. You give us permission to store, copy,
process and show it only as needed to run Studafy for you and your school,
as the Privacy Policy describes. Work handed in to a class, marks, attendance
and files a teacher shares become part of the school's records and may be
kept by the school after your account is deleted.

## 8. The AI study tools

The study helper, whiteboard-to-notes, lesson summaries and study notebooks,
and quizzes and flashcards use an AI provider outside Studafy. The Privacy
Policy says exactly what is sent to it.

- These tools are optional, and are available only where we have switched
  them on.
- **What they produce can be wrong.** Check it before relying on it, and
  never hand it in as your own work.
- AI never marks work in Studafy.
- Each plan has an allowance. We may change the allowances, or pause a tool,
  to keep the service working and affordable.

## 9. Subscriptions

- **Studafy Plus** and **Studafy Max** are for students. **Family+** is for
  parents: one subscription for up to 4 linked children, or a larger one for
  up to 8. Moving to the larger plan upgrades the same subscription. The app
  shows what each one includes, and the store shows the price where you
  live.
- Subscriptions are sold and billed by **Apple** or **Google** under their
  terms, renew every month until cancelled, and are cancelled or refunded
  through your App Store or Google Play account. We never see your card.
- Lessons, the notebook, set work, marks and attendance are free.
- If a subscription ends, the features it unlocked stop. Your notes stay
  yours.

## 10. Meetings

Studafy does not host video calls. When a teacher arranges a meeting, the
invitation is sent through Google Calendar and the call takes place in
Google Meet under Google's terms.

## 11. Availability and changes

We work to keep Studafy available and your data safe, and we take encrypted
backups daily, but we do not promise the service will be uninterrupted or
error-free. We may change or withdraw features. Where a change to these terms
materially affects you, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect
and, where the law requires it, ask you to accept again.

## 12. Ending your use

You can delete your account at any time from Account settings, or by asking
us (see [Delete your account](delete-account)); deletion can be cancelled for
14 days. A parent does this for a child account they manage. Records the
school must keep stay with the school, as the app shows you before you
confirm. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, and will
tell you why unless the law or a child's safety prevents it.
