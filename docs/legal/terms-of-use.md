## 1. What Studafy is

Studafy is a school application. Teachers run classes, share lessons, set and
mark work — including work done on paper, and with suggested marks from AI
if they choose — and record attendance. Students
see and hand in their work and keep their own notes. A student can also use
Studafy on their own, without a class. Parents follow their child's
progress. Everyone can message the people in their classes.

The app has three roles: **teacher**, **student** and **parent or
guardian**. **There is no school administrator inside the app.** A class's
work, marks and attendance are managed by that class's own teachers.
Separately, a school can use the **Studafy School Portal**, a website, to
read the classes of teachers who agree to link with it (§4, §15).

## 2. Accounts

- You sign in with Google, Microsoft or Apple. Keep that account secure; what
  is done with your Studafy account is your responsibility.
- You may add several accounts to one device and switch between them. Sign
  out of any account on a device you share.
- **Child accounts.** A parent or legal guardian may create a student account
  for a child who has no email or phone. By doing so, the parent confirms
  they are the child's parent or legal guardian, accepts these terms and the
  Privacy Policy on the child's behalf, and is responsible for how the
  account is used. The AI study tools are off for such an account unless
  the parent turns them on, and the parent can turn them off at any time
  (§8).
- A student can subscribe to a study plan in the app (§9).
- A profile photo you add stays on your device; it is never uploaded.
- Give accurate information. Do not create an account for someone else
  except as a parent under this section, and do not impersonate anyone.

## 3. Using Studafy properly

You agree not to:

- use Studafy for anything other than learning, teaching and school
  communication;
- harass, bully, threaten or demean anyone, or share anything sexual,
  violent, hateful or unlawful;
- upload files you have no right to share, or that contain malware;
- try to see records you are not entitled to see — another student's marks
  or work, another class, another school — or to get around the app's limits;
- hand in another person's work, or work made by the AI tools, as your own;
- copy, scrape or resell the service or its content.

Anyone can report a message, a conversation or a person, or block someone,
from inside the app. **Studafy reviews reports**: there is no school
administrator inside the app, so reports come to a named person at Studafy, who can be reached at hello@studafy.com. We may remove content,
warn someone, and suspend or close accounts that break these rules,
particularly where a child's safety is at risk. The person who made a
report is told when it has been resolved.

## 4. For teachers

- **You are responsible for the accuracy of the marks and attendance you
  record.** Marks you enter are drafts, visible only to your class's
  teachers, until you publish them. Publishing tells the students and their
  linked parents.
- **Suggested marks.** With Studafy AI Marking (§9) you can ask AI to
  suggest marks for written answers and hand-ins, against a reference you
  write. A suggestion is only a suggestion: it becomes a mark only when you
  save one, and **the mark is yours**. Read the student's work and check
  each suggestion before you save a mark; it can be wrong. Students cannot
  turn suggested marks off, so it is for you to decide when to ask for
  them. Do not put other people's personal details in your reference
  answers.
- **Corrections are recorded, not erased.** To change a published mark you
  must give a reason. The previous mark, the new one, who changed it, when
  and why are kept, and the family is told if the mark itself changed. Give
  honest reasons: they form part of the student's record.
- **Attendance.** You can change marks freely on the lesson's day. After
  that, changing a mark needs a reason. Seven days after the lesson the
  register locks; only the class's lead teacher can then change it, with a
  reason. Every change is kept.
- **Deadlines.** Due times are set in your school's time zone. You may give a
  student more time, or reopen work for the class, and should record why.
- **Lessons and material.** You may edit a lesson or replace its file, and
  change the files on set work. Files you replace or remove are kept for the
  school; the class no longer sees them.
- Share only material you have the right to share, and nothing about a
  student with anyone who has no need to know it.
- **Linking with a school.** A school may ask you, by your Studafy ID, to
  link with it on the Studafy School Portal. If you accept, the school's
  administrators can read, but not change, all your classes: students' and
  linked parents' names and emails, lessons and files, work and answers,
  every mark (drafts and suggested marks included), attendance and the
  wellbeing notes you have not restricted to safeguarding (Privacy Policy
  §7.4). **Studafy does not check that a school is real.** Accept only a
  school you know and are entitled to share your classes with, and report
  one that is not. You can link with one school at a time and unlink at any
  time.
- **Class groups.** A linked school may ask to put one of your classes in a
  group that students join with one code. The class joins only if you
  approve, and you can take it out again. Students who join through a group
  join your class like any other student, and you can remove them.

## 5. For students

- **Joining a class is optional.** You can use your personal notebook and
  the AI study tools on your own, and join a class later with a link or
  code from your teacher.
- Your personal notebook belongs to no class. Only you can see it, not a
  teacher and not a parent, and joining a class does not change it. You can
  edit and rename your notes.
- Hand in your own work. You can replace your work until the deadline shown,
  which is in your school's time zone. Your earlier hand-ins are kept, and
  your teacher can see them.
- After the deadline you cannot hand in unless your teacher gives you more
  time. Once your teacher has published a mark for a piece of work, you cannot
  replace it unless your teacher lets you.
- If you think a mark or an attendance record is wrong, ask your teacher.
- If your teacher links with a school on the Studafy School Portal, that
  school can read your class's records, but cannot change them or message
  you. It can send announcements to your class. A school may give you one
  code that joins several classes at once; it works like a class code.
- Your teacher may ask AI to suggest a mark for your written work; your
  teacher decides the mark. Names Studafy knows are replaced before your
  work is sent, and only your teachers see the suggestion. There is no
  setting to turn this off.

## 6. For parents and guardians

- You can link to your child only with your child's approval, unless you
  created your child's account.
- For a child account you create, the AI study tools are off unless you
  turn them on, when you create the account or later on your child's card
  in your account. You can turn them off at any time (§8).
- A linked parent can see their child's published marks, attendance, set work
  and hand-ins, and class material shared with families, and can download the
  files in them. You can hand work in for a young child who has no device of
  their own; the teacher will see that a parent handed it in.
- **You cannot change your child's marks or attendance**, and you are not
  given your child's private messages.
- If your child's teacher links with a school on the Studafy School Portal,
  the school can read your child's records in that teacher's classes, and
  your name and email as a linked parent, and can send announcements to
  the class's parents.
- **Family+** is an optional paid plan that you hold yourself. One plan
  covers up to 4 of your linked children and a larger plan up to 8, in the
  order you linked them. It summarises what you can already see about each
  child it covers, compares a child only with their own earlier record, and
  does not predict or diagnose anything.

## 7. Your content

You keep the rights you have in what you upload and write — work, notes,
photos, voice notes and messages. You give us permission to store, copy,
process and show it only as needed to run Studafy for you and your school,
as the Privacy Policy describes. Work handed in to a class, marks, attendance
and files a teacher shares become part of the school's records and may be
kept by the school after your account is deleted.

## 8. The AI study tools

The AI Tutor, notes from photos and lessons (whiteboard-to-notes, notes
from a photo or screenshot, and lesson summaries), study notebooks, and
quizzes and flashcards use an AI provider outside Studafy, Together AI. The
Privacy Policy says exactly what is sent to it.

- These tools are optional. They are for every student account, whether or
  not it belongs to a class, and only after the student agrees to them
  separately; for a child account a parent creates, they are off unless
  the parent turns them on, and the parent can turn them off at any time.
  Parent and teacher accounts cannot use them. They can be turned off at
  any time.
- Names Studafy knows are replaced before anything is sent, but photos are
  sent as they are. Do not photograph names or faces, and do not type other
  people's personal details.
- **What they produce can be wrong.** Check it before relying on it, and
  never hand it in as your own work.
- **AI never decides a mark.** A teacher may ask AI for a suggested mark on
  a student's written work, which only teachers see; the teacher decides the
  mark. There is no setting to turn this off (§4, §5; Privacy Policy
  §6.1).
- Each plan has an allowance, which renews every week on the free plan and
  every month on the paid plans. We may change the allowances, or pause a
  tool, to keep the service working and affordable.

## 9. Subscriptions

- **Studafy Plus** and **Studafy Max** are for students, and a student can
  subscribe to either in the app. **Family+** is for parents: one
  subscription for up to 4 linked children, or a larger one for up to 8.
  Moving to the larger plan upgrades the same subscription. **Studafy AI
  Marking** is for teachers: suggested marks for written answers and
  hand-ins (§4), with a monthly allowance. A teacher subscribes for

- **Who pays.** Payment is made through **Google Play** or the **App Store**,

  Studafy does not ask a parent to approve a purchase in the app.
- **Children.** For a child, that store account and its payment method
  should belong to, or be controlled by, a parent or guardian, who is
  responsible for purchases made with it. Google Family Link and Apple's Ask
  to Buy let a parent approve each purchase before it is made.
- Subscriptions are sold and billed by **Apple** or **Google** under their
  terms and renew every month until cancelled in the store. Refunds follow

confirm. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, and will
tell you why unless the law or a child's safety prevents it.

## 15. Schools and the School Portal

The Studafy School Portal (school.studafy.com) lets a school read the
classes of teachers who link with it. These terms and the Privacy Policy
apply to it. This section is for a school's administrators.

- **Who may use it.** An adult (18 or over) who is authorised by the school
  to act for it. By creating a school's page, or accepting an invitation to
  run one, you confirm both. A school's page has up to three
  administrators.
- **Be who you say you are.** Give the school's real name and details. Do
  not create a page for a school you do not represent, or pretend to be
  someone you are not.
- **Ask fairly.** Ask only teachers who teach for your school to link, and
  never pressure a teacher to accept. A teacher can decline, unlink at any
  time and report your school.
- **Use what you see only for the school.** Use it only for the school's
  education and safeguarding purposes, keep it confidential, and share it
  only with people at the school who need it. Do not try to see classes or
  students you are not shown, or to get around the portal's limits.
- **Downloads are your school's responsibility.** A file you download or
  print leaves Studafy. Keep it secure, share it only as the school's
  duties allow, and delete it when it is no longer needed.
- **Announcements.** Send only school matters, to whole classes. Nothing
  that singles out a student, nothing unlawful or inappropriate for
  children, and no advertising.
- **Class groups.** Put together only classes that belong together. Each
  teacher decides whether their class joins.
- **The school is responsible** for its own use of what it reads, as a
  controller under data-protection law, including answering requests from
  students and families about the data it holds.
- **What we may do.** Everything an administrator opens or downloads is
  recorded. We may remove an announcement, and suspend or close a school's
  page that breaks these terms or misuses the portal; a suspended page sees
  nothing. When the last administrator leaves, or a page is closed, every
  teacher is unlinked.
